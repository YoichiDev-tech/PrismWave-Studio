# PrismWave Studio — Company Operating System

This directory defines the operating model, role charters, decision rights, and working standards for PrismWave Studio.

## Mission
Build and deliver reliable, useful digital products and services for businesses, initially focusing on the Italian market. Sell only work the studio can competently deliver, scope honestly, and support after launch.

## Leadership
- **CEO / Founder:** final decision-maker and owner of company direction.
- **COO:** coordinates business operations, delivery, people, client experience, and execution.
- **CTO:** owns technical strategy, engineering quality, architecture, security engineering, and technical delivery.

The CEO works directly with the COO and CTO. They coordinate the organisation and bring concise recommendations, options, risks, costs, and approval requests.

## Lean-startup staffing model
These are **responsibility seats**, not a claim that PrismWave currently employs a full department. In the early stage, one qualified person or AI-assisted workflow may cover multiple compatible seats. Regulated, independent or high-risk work (accounting, tax, legal advice, formal security testing) must be reviewed or performed by appropriately qualified external professionals.

Activate a role when its work exists; keep future roles documented but dormant until needed. Avoid unnecessary bureaucracy.

## Mandatory operating principles
1. **CEO authority:** the CEO has final authority over strategy, positioning, budgets, pricing policy, contracts, hiring, material risk acceptance, production launch, and company commitments.
2. **No self-approval:** a role may prepare work within its remit, but cannot approve its own high-impact or irreversible action.
3. **No invented proof:** never fabricate clients, testimonials, certifications, metrics, case studies, credentials, or delivery capacity.
4. **Honest scope:** only sell services that can be delivered, tested, maintained, and supported with current capability or a disclosed qualified partner.
5. **Evidence first:** decisions and claims should link to source code, tests, customer evidence, signed agreements, invoices, or authoritative references.
6. **Security by default:** never expose credentials, customer data, private prospect data, or internal secrets in this public repository.
7. **Privacy by design:** collect only necessary personal data, define purpose and retention, restrict access, and escalate uncertain GDPR matters to qualified counsel.
8. **Quality gates:** no production release without documented scope, review, relevant tests, rollback plan, and CEO approval.
9. **Client respect:** no spam, deceptive outreach, pressure tactics, or unapproved commitments.
10. **Traceability:** record decisions, assumptions, owners, due dates, and outcomes.

## Decision and escalation rules
See [Decision Rights](governance/DECISION-RIGHTS.md) and [Operating Rhythm](governance/OPERATING-RHYTHM.md).

Stop and escalate when a request changes agreed scope, price, deadline, legal terms, public claims, or creates material security, privacy, financial, legal, or reputational risk; requires production access, destructive data changes, or an irreversible action; or exceeds documented capability, authority, budget, or acceptance criteria.

## AI and automation boundary
AI role agents are assistants, not legal entities, employees, licensed professionals, or autonomous executives. They may research, draft, analyse, implement in approved branches, and prepare recommendations. They must identify uncertainty, cite evidence, request human review where required, and never impersonate a real person or claim to have completed work they did not verify.

## Language
Internal technical and governance documentation is maintained in English for consistency. Customer-facing material for the Italian market should be written in natural, reviewed Italian unless the client requests another language.
