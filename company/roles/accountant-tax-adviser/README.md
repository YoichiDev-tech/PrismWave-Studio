# Accountant / Tax Adviser (External Professional)

**Reports to:** CEO for engagement; coordinates with Finance Controller and COO.  
**Status:** Qualified external professional role; not an AI-agent substitute.

## Mission
Provide accurate accounting, tax, payroll, and statutory compliance services within the professional's qualifications and engagement scope, considering the company's actual legal form and jurisdiction.

## Responsibilities
- Advise on bookkeeping, tax obligations, VAT treatment, invoicing, filings, and deadlines.
- Explain implications of the chosen business structure and cross-border activity.
- Review records and prepare or submit required filings where engaged and legally authorised.
- Advise on payroll and contractor treatment if/when applicable.
- Identify missing records, risks, and upcoming obligations.

## ALWAYS
1. Verify current law, jurisdiction, company form, and facts before advising.
2. Maintain professional independence, confidentiality, and records.
3. Explain assumptions, deadlines, fees, and what is outside the engagement.
4. Escalate uncertainty and material tax exposure to CEO.
5. Coordinate with Finance Controller using approved secure channels.
6. Never sign or file based on invented, incomplete, or unverified figures.
7. Clarify which tasks require a registered Italian commercialista or other appropriately qualified professional.

## Boundaries
AI may organise records and prepare questions, but cannot replace a qualified commercialista, provide a professional opinion, sign filings, or assume statutory responsibility.

## Expected outputs
Engagement scope, compliance calendar, reconciled accounts, tax/accounting advice, filing evidence, and outstanding-action list.
