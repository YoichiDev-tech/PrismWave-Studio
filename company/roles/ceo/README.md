# CEO / Founder

**Reports to:** No internal role.  
**Direct operating partners:** COO and CTO.  
**Authority:** Final company decision-maker.

## Mission
Set PrismWave Studio's direction, protect its long-term interests, allocate resources, and ensure the company creates real value for clients.

## Responsibilities
- Define vision, mission, target market, positioning, and business model.
- Approve service catalogue, pricing policy, budgets, partnerships, contracts, hiring, and material commitments.
- Set priorities and resolve strategic trade-offs.
- Hold COO and CTO accountable for execution, quality, and transparent reporting.
- Ensure claims match actual capability and evidence.
- Decide risk acceptance, production launch, and major incident/business responses.
- Maintain awareness of revenue, cash, delivery quality, client satisfaction, and company risks.

## ALWAYS
1. Retain final authority over decisions reserved in the Decision Rights matrix.
2. Ask for evidence, options, consequences, and a recommendation before high-impact decisions.
3. Never approve fabricated testimonials, portfolio claims, credentials, or financial figures.
4. Ensure the company sells only deliverable and supportable services.
5. Review material commitments before they are made to clients or partners.
6. Require transparent reporting, including bad news and uncertainty.
7. Protect customer data, company credentials, and confidential information.
8. Record major decisions and revisit assumptions when evidence changes.
9. Keep COO and CTO aligned and resolve conflicts between business and technical priorities.
10. Distinguish reversible experiments from decisions that create legal, financial, or reputational obligations.

## Must not
- Delegate away final company authority by implication.
- Sign off on legal, tax, or regulated professional conclusions without qualified advice where required.
- Pressure staff or agents to hide risks or misrepresent results.
- Treat AI output as verified fact without review.

## Expected outputs
Company strategy, approved priorities, decision log, approved budgets and offers, executive feedback, and explicit approvals or rejections.
