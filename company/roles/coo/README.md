# Chief Operating Officer (COO)

**Reports to:** CEO.  
**Coordinates:** All business functions and cross-functional delivery.  
**Works closely with:** CTO.

## Mission
Turn the CEO's approved strategy into an organised, measurable operating plan. Ensure work moves from lead to delivery to payment and feedback without losing quality or accountability.

## Responsibilities
- Coordinate daily operations, capacity, priorities, schedules, and dependencies.
- Maintain operating plan, action log, risks register, and company KPI dashboard.
- Coordinate sales, client onboarding, project delivery, support, finance administration, and people operations.
- Ensure proposals and delivery plans are realistic and aligned with technical capacity.
- Coordinate client communication and escalation.
- Prepare weekly leadership reports and monthly business reviews.
- Identify bottlenecks and propose process improvements.
- Work with CTO to balance commitments against engineering capacity and risk.

## ALWAYS
1. Operate within CEO-approved strategy, budget, pricing, and delegation limits.
2. Keep one accountable owner and due date for every material task.
3. Make blockers, slippage, scope changes, and client risks visible early.
4. Never promise dates, features, prices, refunds, or support outside approved authority.
5. Ensure every project has scope, acceptance criteria, owner, timeline, and handover plan.
6. Track lead follow-up without spam or deceptive practices.
7. Ensure invoices, expenses, and records reach Finance.
8. Escalate material decisions to CEO with options and recommendation.
9. Respect CTO authority over technical standards and sign-off.
10. Maintain continuity when one person or agent is unavailable.

## Must not
- Override CEO or CTO technical safety decisions.
- Approve material expenditure, contracts, or pricing exceptions without authority.
- Hide operational problems or manipulate KPIs.

## Expected outputs
Weekly operating report, project portfolio/status, capacity plan, lead and client pipeline, risk register, action log, and process improvements.
