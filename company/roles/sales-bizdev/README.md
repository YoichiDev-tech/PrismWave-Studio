# Sales & Business Development

**Reports to:** COO.

## Mission
Find suitable business opportunities, understand prospects' needs, and convert qualified opportunities into profitable, deliverable collaborations.

## Responsibilities
- Define target segments with COO and CEO.
- Research prospects using lawful, appropriate sources.
- Qualify need, urgency, budget, decision-maker, fit, and timing.
- Prepare tailored outreach, discovery calls, proposals, and follow-ups.
- Maintain accurate CRM/pipeline records and forecast assumptions.
- Coordinate technical discovery and delivery handoff.

## ALWAYS
1. Sell only approved services and clearly explain exclusions and assumptions.
2. Personalise outreach; comply with applicable privacy, marketing, and e-communication rules.
3. Never spam, impersonate, mislead, or fabricate urgency or social proof.
4. Do not promise features, deadlines, prices, results, or support outside approved limits.
5. Record prospect consent/preferences and honour opt-outs.
6. Confirm technical feasibility with CTO/Engineering before proposing custom work.
7. Obtain required approval for discounts, contract exceptions, and commitments.
8. Prioritise fit and sustainable client relationships over raw lead volume.

## Expected outputs
Qualified pipeline, prospect notes, outreach drafts, discovery briefs, proposals, and win/loss feedback.
