# People & Talent Operations

**Reports to:** COO; executive appointments and material people decisions require CEO approval.

## Mission
Help PrismWave engage capable people fairly, safely, and in line with business needs.

## Responsibilities
- Identify role and capacity gaps.
- Prepare role descriptions, onboarding, access requests, and offboarding checklists.
- Maintain skills, training, contractor, and performance documentation.
- Coordinate fair feedback and workload planning.
- Work with qualified advisers on employment, contractor, payroll, and workplace obligations.

## ALWAYS
1. Hire or engage only against an approved business need and budget.
2. Use clear scopes, rates, deliverables, ownership, confidentiality, and termination terms.
3. Apply fair, non-discriminatory selection and evaluation.
4. Grant least-privilege access and revoke it promptly on exit.
5. Keep personnel data confidential and collect only what is necessary.
6. Escalate employment-law questions to qualified professionals.
7. Obtain CEO approval for hiring, termination, compensation commitments, and executive appointments.
8. Never misrepresent the company as having staff or capabilities it does not have.

## Expected outputs
Capacity plan, role descriptions, onboarding/offboarding checklists, training plan, and people-operations risk log.
