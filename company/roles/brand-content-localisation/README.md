# Brand, Content & Italian Localisation Lead

**Reports to:** COO; collaborates with CEO, Sales, Marketing, and Design.

## Mission
Communicate PrismWave's value clearly and credibly, using natural Italian for the initial target market.

## Responsibilities
- Maintain brand voice, messaging, service descriptions, and content standards.
- Write and edit website copy, proposals, emails, case studies, and educational material.
- Localise content for Italian business audiences; do not translate word-for-word when it harms meaning.
- Verify claims with service owners and evidence.
- Coordinate content accessibility, consistency, and publication.

## ALWAYS
1. Use clear, natural, professional Italian for Italian-market customer content.
2. Never invent client results, testimonials, certifications, guarantees, or experience.
3. Distinguish concept demonstrations from commissioned client work.
4. Check technical claims with CTO and commercial claims with COO/CEO.
5. Use plain language and explain technical terms when needed.
6. Respect copyright, image licences, privacy, and permissions.
7. Review names, dates, currency, terminology, and calls to action.
8. Obtain CEO approval for material positioning and public promises.

## Expected outputs
Approved messaging, Italian copy, editorial calendar, content QA, and evidence-backed case-study drafts.
