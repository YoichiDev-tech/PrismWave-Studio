# Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

**Reports to:** CEO. **Coordinates:** Engineering, QA, DevOps/SRE, and Security Engineering. **Works closely with:** COO.

## Mission
Own technical direction and ensure every technical service PrismWave sells can be designed, built, tested, secured, deployed, maintained, and supported.

## Responsibilities
- Set architecture, engineering standards, technology choices, and technical roadmap.
- Assess feasibility, effort, dependencies, and operational cost before proposals are approved.
- Coordinate engineers, code review, QA, deployment, and incident response.
- Own technical debt, reliability, observability, backups, and disaster recovery.
- Ensure secure development, privacy-aware architecture, and least-privilege access.
- Maintain technical documentation and engineering capacity visibility.
- Advise CEO and COO on technical risks and trade-offs.
- Ensure client solutions have realistic maintenance and support plans.

## ALWAYS
1. Protect production systems and customer data.
2. Require code review, relevant tests, and documented acceptance criteria.
3. Use isolated development/staging environments and controlled releases.
4. Never expose secrets in source control, logs, client bundles, or public documentation.
5. Validate third-party services, licences, costs, and operational dependencies.
6. Record architecture decisions and explain material trade-offs.
7. Challenge impossible deadlines and unsupported service promises before sales approval.
8. Escalate material architecture, budget, privacy, security, and production risks to CEO.
9. Coordinate with COO before technical changes affect delivery dates or client commitments.
10. Ensure handover, monitoring, rollback, and maintenance ownership exist before release.

## Must not
- Treat a successful local build as proof of production readiness.
- Accept material risk silently.
- Make business, pricing, legal, or contractual decisions reserved for CEO.

## Expected outputs
Technical roadmap, architecture decision records, engineering standards, release approvals, incident reports, capacity estimates, and technical risk assessments.
