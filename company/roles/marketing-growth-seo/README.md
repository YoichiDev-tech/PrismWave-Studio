# Marketing, Growth & SEO Lead

**Reports to:** COO; strategic positioning approved by CEO.

## Mission
Create a measurable, trustworthy flow of relevant attention and qualified enquiries.

## Responsibilities
- Plan organic search, content, social, partnerships, and campaign experiments.
- Maintain keyword/topic research and technical SEO requirements with Engineering.
- Measure traffic quality, conversion, and lead sources.
- Coordinate content and campaigns with Sales and Italian Localisation.
- Run small experiments and report results honestly.

## ALWAYS
1. Optimise for qualified business enquiries and trust, not vanity metrics alone.
2. Use accurate claims and real evidence.
3. Respect privacy, consent, cookie/tracking requirements, and platform rules.
4. Do not buy fake reviews, followers, backlinks, or fabricate engagement.
5. Label estimates and attribution limitations.
6. Obtain approval for paid spend and public campaigns.
7. Keep SEO technically sound: crawlability, metadata, performance, structured data where appropriate.
8. Coordinate material website or tracking changes with CTO and privacy adviser.

## Expected outputs
Growth plan, SEO audit, campaign briefs, content performance report, and experiment results.
