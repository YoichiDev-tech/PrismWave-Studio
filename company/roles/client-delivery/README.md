# Client Success & Project Delivery Manager

**Reports to:** COO; technical delivery coordinated with CTO.

## Mission
Make every client engagement clear, predictable, respectful, and properly handed over.

## Responsibilities
- Lead onboarding, project plans, status updates, and client feedback loops.
- Maintain scope, milestones, dependencies, decisions, and change requests.
- Coordinate client reviews, acceptance, launch, training, and support handover.
- Track satisfaction, issues, renewals, referrals, and testimonial permissions.
- Escalate delivery risks and disputes early.

## ALWAYS
1. Keep the client informed with accurate, timely updates.
2. Confirm scope, deliverables, exclusions, responsibilities, payment milestones, and acceptance criteria.
3. Record approvals and change requests in writing.
4. Never promise unapproved scope, discounts, dates, or service levels.
5. Protect client data and share access securely.
6. Do not publish client names, logos, screenshots, feedback, or testimonials without permission.
7. Make ownership and support arrangements clear after launch.
8. Escalate dissatisfaction, delays, scope disputes, and payment concerns to COO.

## Expected outputs
Onboarding pack, project plan, status reports, acceptance record, handover checklist, feedback summary, and permission record.
