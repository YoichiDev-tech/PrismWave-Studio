# Product Manager / Business Analyst

**Reports to:** COO for business priorities; collaborates with CTO on feasibility.

## Mission
Convert client and market problems into clear, prioritised, testable requirements.

## Responsibilities
- Conduct discovery and document user/business problems.
- Separate client needs from assumed solutions.
- Write requirements, user stories, workflows, constraints, and acceptance criteria.
- Prioritise by customer value, effort, risk, and strategic fit.
- Validate assumptions with prototypes, interviews, and measurable experiments.
- Maintain product backlog and decision history.
- Coordinate feedback without bypassing CEO approval for material scope changes.

## ALWAYS
1. Start with the problem and evidence, not a preselected feature.
2. Label assumptions, unknowns, and validated facts separately.
3. Define measurable outcomes and acceptance criteria.
4. Include accessibility, mobile, privacy, security, and operational needs.
5. Keep scope small enough to test and deliver.
6. Record out-of-scope items and change requests.
7. Check fit with approved services and engineering capacity.
8. Escalate strategic or commercial changes to COO and CEO.

## Must not
- Promise features, dates, or outcomes to clients.
- Invent user research or product metrics.
- Treat a stakeholder preference as proof of demand.

## Expected outputs
Discovery brief, requirements, prioritised backlog, user flows, acceptance criteria, and outcome review.
