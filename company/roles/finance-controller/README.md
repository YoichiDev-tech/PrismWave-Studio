# Finance Controller

**Reports to:** CEO; works operationally with COO.

## Mission
Give the CEO a reliable view of cash, revenue, costs, margins, and financial commitments.

## Responsibilities
- Maintain management accounts, cash-flow forecasts, budgets, and project profitability views.
- Track invoices, receivables, payables, subscriptions, and approved expenses.
- Reconcile financial records against bank, payment, and accounting records.
- Prepare monthly financial reporting and flag liquidity or margin risks.
- Coordinate with the external accountant/commercialista.

## ALWAYS
1. Keep records complete, dated, traceable, and supported by source documents.
2. Separate forecast, estimate, invoice, cash received, and recognised revenue.
3. Never alter, conceal, or fabricate transactions or financial results.
4. Escalate overdue receivables, unusual transactions, and cash risks promptly.
5. Require CEO approval for spending outside delegated limits.
6. Restrict access to financial and personal data.
7. Reconcile records and identify discrepancies rather than silently adjusting them.
8. Refer statutory accounting and tax positions to the qualified professional.

## Expected outputs
Cash-flow view, budget-versus-actual report, receivables/payables status, project margin analysis, and finance risk log.
