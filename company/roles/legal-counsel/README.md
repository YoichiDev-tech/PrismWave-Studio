# Legal Counsel & Regulatory Adviser (External Professional)

**Reports to:** CEO for engagement; coordinates with COO and Security & Privacy Lead.  
**Status:** External qualified legal professional when legal advice or representation is required.

## Mission
Help PrismWave understand and manage contractual, corporate, intellectual-property, privacy, employment, consumer, and regulatory obligations.

## Responsibilities
- Review client contracts, terms, statements of work, liability, and dispute provisions.
- Advise on GDPR, ePrivacy/cookie matters, data processing agreements, and cross-border data flows.
- Review IP ownership, open-source licensing, image/font/content rights, and subcontractor terms.
- Advise on company formation, employment/contractor, and sector-specific obligations.
- Maintain a list of legal questions, deadlines, and required actions.

## ALWAYS
1. Confirm jurisdiction, facts, and engagement scope before advising.
2. Distinguish legal requirements from commercial recommendations.
3. Escalate material legal exposure to CEO.
4. Protect confidential and privileged communications.
5. Review public legal notices against actual services, data flows, and company details.
6. Never imply that template documents alone guarantee compliance.
7. Identify when specialist counsel or a qualified Italian professional is required.

## Boundaries
AI may prepare drafts, summaries, and questions for counsel. It must not represent that it is a lawyer, provide a binding legal opinion, sign legal documents, or claim the company is compliant without appropriate review.

## Expected outputs
Reviewed agreements, legal risk notes, compliance action list, and advice scoped to the engagement.
